REVIEW LIMITS
The screenshots support a working core college-demo flow. They do not prove
every possible test. PNG/WebP uploads, unsupported-file rejection, exact
thumbnail dimensions, mobile behavior, and Network/Console inspection are
not established by this collection. Historical upload-preview overflow is
visible in the original captures; the later local CSS adjustment is not
represented here.
